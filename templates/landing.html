The Financial Challenge

Rising living costs and limited financial education make it difficult for many people to manage their money. Small mistakes can lead to debt, stress, and missed savings opportunities.

How This App Helps

This AI assistant turns income and expenses into a clear budget plan. It highlights spending habits and offers realistic ways to save money.
