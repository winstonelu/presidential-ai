Community Budget Assistant

A simple AI-powered tool designed to help people understand their money, reduce unnecessary spending, and build better financial habits—no math skills required.

The Problem

Many people struggle to manage their finances due to rising living costs, limited financial education, and confusing budgeting tools. This often leads to overspending, missed savings goals, and financial stress.

The Solution

The Community Budget Assistant uses artificial intelligence to turn income, expenses, and savings goals into a clear and friendly budget plan. It explains where your money goes and shows practical ways to improve your situation.

Why This App Helps

Easy to Use

Users type their income and expenses in one sentence and get instant feedback.

Accessible for All Ages

Designed for children, young adults, and elderly users with clear language.

Practical Advice

Focuses on realistic savings tips instead of complex financial terms.

Community Impact

Helps reduce financial mistakes and encourages smarter money habits.

Built for the Presidential AI Challenge • Empowering financial literacy through AI
